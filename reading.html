What these things actually are

Three lenses. One moment.

All three systems start from the same instant: the minute you arrived, seen from the place you arrived. Astrology reads the sky. Human Design reads the same planets as a map of how your energy moves and how you decide. The Gene Keys read the same numbers as a path of contemplation, from shadow to gift. Read separately they are three vocabularies. Read together they are one blueprint, and the places where all three name the same thing are the whole point. Tap a lens.

Lens one · the sky

Astrology

A map of the sky at the minute you were born, seen from the place you were born. The planets are the functions: the drive, the feeling body, the voice, the will. The signs are the style each one works in. The houses are the twelve rooms of a life where each one plays out. And the aspects are the conversations between them, measured in degrees: the closer to exact, the louder.
